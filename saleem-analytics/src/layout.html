Month by month

MoM and YoY use orders per day, so months of different length and the current partial month compare fairly. The orange tag shows how many Ramadan days fell in that month, since Ramadan moves about 11 days earlier each year and distorts YoY.

Service breakdown

Previous period = the same number of days immediately before. Last year = the same dates shifted back 364 days, so weekdays line up. Ad spend here is spend on campaigns for that service only; general, brand and app campaigns are shown in the ads section.

How the selected services move together

Each cell is the correlation of week-to-week changes between the row and the column service, in the same week (1 = always together, 0 = unrelated, negative = opposite). A note shows when the column service moves a few weeks after or before the row service more strongly than in the same week.

Weekly volume side by side

Each service is shown as a share of its own weekly average (100 = a normal week), so services of very different size can be compared on one scale. Hover for the actual numbers.

Does one lead the other?

Correlation of week-over-week changes, with the second service shifted earlier or later. A bar well above zero at +1 means a rise in the first service is usually followed by a rise in the second a week later.

    Same-week links are often shared causes (holidays, ad pushes, salary days) rather than one service driving the other. A link that shows up one or two weeks later is the better sign of one service feeding another. Correlation is not proof of cause.

    One per 100 of the other

    The smaller service per 100 of the larger, by month. A rising line means the smaller service is growing faster, for example more lab tests for every 100 doctor visits.

    Same patients, next service

    Share of patients who had the first service in a month and then the second within 30 days. This is the direct measure of one service leading to another.

    This needs the service follow-on export. Open the Data tab, copy the follow-on SQL, run it in Metabase and drop the CSV there.

    Unique patients

    Each patient counted once per month, by the month of their visit (scheduled time) whatever Dates by is set to. New patients follow the New patient = switch in the filter bar: first-ever visit in that month, or patient record created in that month. Returning = everyone else served that month.

    This section needs one extra Metabase export. The main export counts patients per day, so unique patients can't be worked out from it.

    1. In Metabase, create a new SQL question and paste the query (Copy SQL below).
    2. Run it with no date filter, then download the results as CSV.
    3. Open the Data tab and drop the file on the patients box.

    Unique patients can't be added across months or categories: someone treated in both March and April counts once in each month. For a longer period, compare month by month.

    Meta ads

    Each ad is matched to the service it promotes by its name: first its code (PT, NS, DRV, LT…), then words in the ad name, the ad set name, and finally the campaign name. Recruitment, app-install and awareness campaigns keep their own groups. Corrections go in ad_service_map.csv. Results are counted the way each campaign's objective defines them: WhatsApp conversations for message campaigns, purchases, installs or leads for the others. Cost per order divides spend by orders for the matching services in the same dates, so it is a rough efficiency signal, not attribution.

    Projected orders

      By month

      The current month combines orders so far with the projection for the rest of it.

      By service

      How accurate is it?

      The same model re-run as if it were an earlier date, then compared with what actually happened over the next 28 days. This is where the likely range comes from.

      Holiday effects

      Orders per day during each holiday compared with the same weekdays in the four weeks before and after. Uses the services and statuses selected above and all available history. Grey years had too little volume to count toward the average.

      Outside factor effects

      Events from the Google Sheet (sudden holidays, salary windows, security and economic events), measured the same way as holidays: orders per day during the event against the same weekdays in the four weeks around it. Campaign launches and events longer than two weeks aren't measured.

      Events log

      Everything in the Saleem Outside Factors Google Sheet except official holidays, which come from the calendar. These are the markers on the trend chart. Add new events to the sheet; ask Claude to check the news and it will add them there too.

      Events you add are saved in this browser only. Use "Copy as CSV" and send them to Claude to make them permanent.
